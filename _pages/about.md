Daocheng Fu is a Ph.D. candidate at Fudan University’s College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, in a joint training program with Shanghai AI Laboratory. He is advised by Prof. [Yu Qiao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gFtI-8QAAAAJ&hl=en) and Dr. [Botian Shi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=K0PpvLkAAAAJ&hl=en), and his research focuses on data synthesis, language model reasoning and continual learning.


## News

- [2026-01-13] [TraineeBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08173) establishes a dynamic evaluation framework that simulates realistic workplace scenarios to rigorously assess agents on context-aware scheduling for streaming tasks, active exploration under uncertainty, and continuous learning from experience.
- [2026-01-08] [SGVR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05073) introduces a reinforcement learning framework that replaces sparse outcome-based supervision with dense, verifiable sub-goal rewards derived from formal proof skeletons. By guiding models through rigorous intermediate milestones, it significantly enhances geometric reasoning performance while demonstrating robust generalization to general mathematics and logical reasoning tasks.
- [2025-12-25] [SymDrive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21618) proposes a unified diffusion-based framework that achieves both photorealistic novel-view synthesis and realistic traffic editing by leveraging a Symmetric Auto-regressive Online Restoration paradigm to recover fine-grained details from symmetric views. Furthermore, it utilizes this restoration capability to enable a training-free harmonization mechanism for seamless, lighting-consistent 3D vehicle insertion into driving scenes.
- [2025-09-30] [RE-Searcher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26048) investigates the relationship between search agents and the search environment, proposing a solution to enhance the agent's stability.
- [2025-08-30] We release the GeoTrust-train & GeoTrust-test dataset at [huggingface](https://huggingface.co/datasets/U4R/GeoTrust).
- [2025-08-29] [TrustGeoGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15780) is a data engine for multimodal geometric reasoning, built upon a formal engine, designed to synthesize challenging, full-modality, and trustworthy geometric reasoning data. The introduction of `Connection Thinking` and `Thinking Template` enhances the geometric reasoning capabilities of Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) without compromising their general abilities.
