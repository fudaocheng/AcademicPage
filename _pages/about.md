Daocheng Fu is a Ph.D. candidate at Fudan University’s College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, in a joint training program with Shanghai AI Laboratory. He is advised by Prof. [Yu Qiao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gFtI-8QAAAAJ&hl=en) and Dr. [Botian Shi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=K0PpvLkAAAAJ&hl=en), and his research focuses on data synthesis and continual learning.
